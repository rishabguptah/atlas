How the Black Death rode the trade network from the Tian Shan to Moscow, month by month.

Arrival months are approximate (Benedictow 2004 for the Black Death; Pollitzer, McGrew and Rosenberg for cholera; Crosby and Cook for smallpox). Plague returns plots 11,000 outbreaks from Biraben (1975), digitised by Krauer and Schmid (2022). Each line joins a city to the place it most likely caught plague from; those links are a model's reading of the trade network and wait for checking. Dashed outlines are the borders of the period.
