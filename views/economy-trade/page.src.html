Where the money moved

The centre of world trade has never stayed put. It passed from Dilmun to New York in the west and from Lothal to Shanghai in the east, and every handover had a cause: a sack, a silted harbour, a new sea route. Drag the year and watch the baton change hands.

India and Britain, four centuries apart and back

In 1600, the year the East India Company was chartered, the land that is now India had an economy about sixteen times the size of the land that is now the United Kingdom, and average incomes were not far apart. Then the gap opened.

How connected the world got

World trade as a share of world output rose through the 1800s, collapsed between the world wars, and only passed its 1913 level again in the 1970s. Before 1800, intercontinental trade per person shows which European states lived off the oceans.
