Era-aware pixel sprites for the Atlas world map, drawn on the map's own palette.

Every sprite knows when and where it belongs. Drag the year to see which army, ship, caravan and city each region gets. The banner colour is swapped per realm at runtime.

Weak spots to fix next

  • The East Asian town and the earthquake are the least readable sprites; both need another pass.
  • Horses share one body, so the knight, horse archer, chariot and wagon all walk the same way. A gallop cycle would help the steppe armies.
  • People face the viewer while armies face right. Right-facing walkers for people would let them travel along routes.
  • Wonders are drawn front-on at one size; the largest ones may want a smaller far-zoom version.
